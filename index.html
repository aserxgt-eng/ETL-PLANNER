Progressive Overload Rules
  • →Each week, add 1-2 reps per set OR 1 extra set
  • →When you can do top end reps easily for 2 weeks, level up the exercise
  • →Week 1-4: Master form at beginner reps
  • →Week 5-8: Move to intermediate reps

// 05 — Academic & Skill Development
STUDY SYSTEM
🍅
Pomodoro Method
The only study system that actually works
  • →25 min — deep focus, phone in another room, one task only
  • →5 min break — stand, stretch, drink water. No social media.
  • →After 4 rounds — 20 min longer break
  • →Do 4–6 Pomodoros per study session (2–3 hours)
  • →Use a physical timer or Forest app — not your phone clock
🧠
Active Recall Method
2–4× more effective than re-reading
  • →Read a section → close book → write what you remember
  • →Convert notes into questions → test yourself daily
  • →Use spaced repetition: review after 1 day, 3 days, 1 week
  • →Teach concepts out loud as if explaining to a friend
  • →Anki app for memorization-heavy subjects (biology, history)
⌨️
Daily Typing Practice (30 min at 8:00 PM)
Month 1–2
  • →Use keybr.com daily
  • →Home row mastery first
  • →Goal: 40 WPM with 95% accuracy
Month 3–4
  • →Switch to monkeytype.com
  • →Time trials: 60s, 120s modes
  • →Goal: 60–70 WPM
Month 5+
  • →10fastfingers.com competitions
  • →Type content scripts to practice
  • →Goal: 80–100 WPM
⚡
Dopamine Control & Anti-Procrastination
  • 🚫No reels/TikTok/shorts before 6 PM
  • 🚫No gaming before study blocks are done
  • 🚫Delete or disable Instagram Explore/Reels
  • ✓Use 2-minute rule: if a task takes <2 min, do it NOW
  • ✓Work rewards: finish study block → 10 min game/call
  • ✓Put phone face-down during study (vibration off)
  • ✓Study with lo-fi music if needed — no songs with lyrics
  • ✓Plan tomorrow the night before — reduces morning decisions

// 09 — Mentality & Discipline
MINDSET RULES
01
Identity first, habits second
Tell yourself: "I am someone who trains, studies, and creates every day." When your identity shifts, the habits follow automatically.
02
Never miss twice
Missing one day is an accident. Missing two is a new habit forming. Always bounce back the next day — no guilt, just action.
03
Win the morning, win the day
If you complete your morning routine (stretch, sunlight, breakfast, study block), the rest of the day is already 60% won.
04
Compare to yesterday's you
Looking at other people's bodies or channels will kill your motivation. The only competition is who you were yesterday.
05
Social media is a tool, not entertainment
Use it to grow your channel, learn, or connect. Set 45-min screen time limit on Instagram/YouTube Shorts (use Screen Time settings).
06
Your girlfriend is your teammate
Share your goals with her. Having someone who believes in you in Canada while you grind in India is a massive strength, not a distraction.
07
Discipline is built, not born
Nobody woke up disciplined. It comes from doing the hard thing when you don't feel like it — for 3 weeks straight, until it's automatic.
08
Progress is invisible until it isn't
Results in fitness, skin, and content take 4–12 weeks to show. Trust the process when you can't see it yet. Your future self is watching.
